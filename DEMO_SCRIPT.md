VayuSetu AI demo script
Target length: 3–5 minutes.
0:00–0:25 — Problem
“Fixed monitoring stations can miss hyper-local pollution events. VayuSetu AI combines community evidence with environmental data so cities can detect, predict, and respond earlier.”
0:25–1:00 — City overview
Select Chennai. Show KPI cards, hotspot map, evidence row, current AQI, and predicted AQI. Explain that demo values are labelled and the live version uses CPCB data.
1:00–1:45 — Citizen report
Open Report Pollution. Submit a waste-burning report from North Chennai. Upload a sample image if available and click Analyse with Gemini Vision. Show detected event, possible source, confidence, nearby AQI, and existing hotspot correlation.
1:45–2:25 — Forecast
Open Forecast. Show the AQI trend from 178 to 198, prediction drivers, and area-wise forecast. Click Notify for the affected area.
2:25–3:05 — Authority response
Open Authority & Alerts. Show priority, expected time, affected radius, recommended response, and alert lifecycle. Acknowledge the alert and assign the response team.
3:05–3:35 — Community response
Return to Overview. Show resident, school, and driver guidance. Click Notify Community.
3:35–4:10 — India-scale network
Open City Network. Show Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, and Indore, shared signals, cross-city patterns, and the privacy explanation.
Closing sentence
“VayuSetu AI turns a citizen’s pollution report into explainable intelligence, an early warning, an authority response, and practical community guidance.”
